/about --:-- -- KOLKATA /

I'm Gaurav—I build at the intersection of hardware and digital design. I bridge the gap between abstract visual concepts and functional, tech-driven systems, focusing on precision and aesthetic impact.

From crafting cinematic visual identities to developing custom electronic systems and automation solutions, I turn technical challenges into refined digital experiences.

GH LI IG
/the process
01

Discovery & Strategy

We begin by deconstructing the problem. This phase is about understanding the core objective, defining the aesthetic direction, and laying the strategic groundwork before any pixels are pushed.

02

Concept & Architecture

Translating strategy into wireframes and visual concepts. I build the architectural skeleton of the digital experience, ensuring that form strictly follows function while maintaining a premium feel.

03

Execution & Refinement

The final phase where aesthetics meet engineering. High-fidelity design, interaction physics, and pixel-perfect development come together to create an immersive, cinematic final product.
