About project

Summer Melt is a playful AI-driven visual project created for LUNA Ice, a fictional ice cream brand. The project captures the essence of warm afternoons, melting treats, and the bittersweet feeling of summer ending.

Project overview

The project was designed as a campaign for an end-of-summer party, capturing the sweet, fleeting mood of long days, pastel skies, and the last scoop of ice cream before autumn arrives.

What I did

I created the campaign concept, generated and curated visuals through AI tools, refined the warm color palette, and designed a cohesive visual identity that evokes nostalgia and joy.
