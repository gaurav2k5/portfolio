About project

Virtual Threads is a conceptual project for NEONWEAR, a fictional fashion label that merges digital culture with physical clothing. The project explores what fashion looks like when it exists equally in virtual and real spaces.

Project overview

The project explores the idea of fashion as an immersive experience, where garments are not just worn but experienced through technology, light, and digital enhancement.

What I did

I developed the creative direction, generated AI-based visuals, and built a narrative around the concept of fashion transcending physical boundaries into the digital realm.
