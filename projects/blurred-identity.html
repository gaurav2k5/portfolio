About project

Blurred Identity is a conceptual AI-generated portrait project for NOEMA Collective. It explores the tension between being seen and remaining invisible — the moments where identity begins to dissolve at the edges.

Project overview

The project is built around the tension between clarity and distortion. Blur is not treated as an error but as an intentional visual language that communicates vulnerability, transition, and the complexity of self-presentation.

What I did

I created the visual concept, experimented with AI tools to achieve layered blur effects, curated the final portraits, and built a narrative framework that positions blur as a form of artistic expression.
